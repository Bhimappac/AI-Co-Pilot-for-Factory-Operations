# SOP-ENV-04: Industrial Coolant Leak Containment and Emergency Response

## 1. Initial Assessment and Hazard Identification
This standard operating procedure defines the immediate containment, cleanup, and reporting protocols for water-soluble and synthetic cutting coolant leaks from machining centers CNC-01 through CNC-08.
**ENVIRONMENTAL & SLIP HAZARD:** Coolant mixtures (specifically Cimcool Cimtech 410) pose high slip-and-fall hazards and potential skin sensitization. Prevent any coolant runoff from entering floor drains or stormwater outlets. Wear chemical splash goggles, neoprene gloves (EN 374), and slip-resistant rubber safety overboots.

## 2. Emergency Isolation and Leak Source Stoppage
When a coolant leak is observed or annunciated by fault code **E-412: Coolant Delivery System Pressure Loss (< 1.5 bar)**:
1. Immediately press the **Feed Hold** button and turn off high-pressure coolant pump selector **SW-PMP-01** at the operator pendant.
2. If the leak originates from a ruptured high-pressure flex hose, press Emergency Stop **PB-01** to de-energize the entire fluid circuit.
3. Close the manual quarter-turn quarter-inch stainless ball valve **V-CLT-SUPPLY** on the main reservoir feeder to isolate fluid delivery.
4. Notify the environmental compliance officer and maintenance lead via plant radio channel 3.

## 3. Spill Containment and Barrier Deployment
1. Retrieve Mobile Spill Response Kit **SK-01** stationed at Bay C column 14.
2. Deploy yellow polypropylene absorbent socks (**SK-SOCK-03**) completely encircling the fluid perimeter to prevent spread toward floor drain **DR-04**.
3. Place absorbent drain seal mat (**MAT-DR-01**) directly over floor drain DR-04 and stomp firmly to create a watertight vacuum seal against the concrete substrate.
4. Surround the leaking machine enclosure with universal absorbent pads (**PAD-UNV-50**), overlapping pad edges by at least 50 mm.
5. If fluid accumulation exceeds 20 liters, deploy the air-operated diaphragm portable transfer pump (**PMP-TRANS-AODD**) and direct the discharge wand into marked polyethylene salvage drum **DRUM-CLT-WASTE**.

## 4. Hose and Fitting Inspection and Repair Verification
1. Inspect high-pressure braided coolant lines (**CH-01** through **CH-04**) for puncture, crimp fitting slippage, or abrasive wear against the machine chassis.
2. Check Quick-Disconnect couplers (**QD-CLT-8**) for degraded Buna-N O-rings. If worn, replace O-ring with part number **OR-BN70-112**.
3. Check filter canister **FLT-CLT-20** for clogged 20-micron filter element. A differential pressure indicator reading in the red zone (> 2.2 bar) indicates filter rupture risks. Replace element with **EL-20M-SYN**.
4. Tighten all flare fittings to 28 Nm using calibrated torque wrench **TW-50**.

## 5. Decontamination, Disposal, and Coolant Recharge
1. Collect all saturated absorbent pads and containment socks using spark-proof brass shovel **SH-BR01**. Place spent absorbents into heavy-duty 6-mil hazardous waste bags **BAG-HAZ-YW**.
2. Label waste bags with "SPENT SYNTHETIC COOLANT - CIMTECH 410" and deposit in designated Hazardous Waste Collection Area H-2.
3. Clean the floor with biodegradable floor degreaser **DEG-BIO-10** and industrial wet-vacuum **VAC-WET-02**. Ensure the surface passes the dry-friction slip test before removing warning cones.
4. Refill coolant reservoir with 8% pre-mixed refractometer-verified coolant (Brix index 8.2 on optical refractometer **RF-OPT-01**).
5. Clear alarm code E-412 on HMI and run coolant pump dry-cycle test for 3 minutes to verify zero line leakage at 25 bar operating pressure.
